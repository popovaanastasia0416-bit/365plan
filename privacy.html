Privacy and cookies

my365plan.com · last updated 28 November 2026

Who we are

my365plan sells and shares printable PDF planners. Contact: support.my365plan@gmail.com.

What we collect

Visit statistics. Cloudflare, which hosts this site, counts visits, pages, referring sites, countries and device types. Its Web Analytics does not use cookies.

Behaviour analytics, only with your consent. If you press “Accept”, Microsoft Clarity records how the page is used (clicks, scrolling, mouse movement, session replays and heatmaps) and sets cookies. Microsoft processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. If you press “Decline”, Clarity is not loaded.

Wheel of life. Your scores and ticked steps are stored only in your browser.

Purchases. Payments are handled by Payhip and PayPal under their own privacy policies. We receive your name, email address and order details to deliver the files and answer questions. We never see your card number.

Emails you send us. We use them to reply.

Why we use it

To deliver purchases, answer you, and learn which pages are useful so we can improve the site. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices and rights

You can change your analytics choice at any time. You may ask us what data we hold about you, and ask to correct or delete it, by writing to the address above. Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland also have the right to object and to complain to their data protection authority.
